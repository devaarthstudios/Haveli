Privacy Policy

Haveli by Devaarth Studios. Last updated: November 23, 2026.

Haveli is a mobile game made by Devaarth Studios. This page explains what happens to your information when you play.

1. Permissions and internet access

The current Haveli build does not request any Android permissions, including the internet permission, and the game itself contains no code that sends data over the internet.

2. Advertising and analytics

A scan of the current build found no known advertising or analytics packages, and the build has no internet permission.

3. Information stored on your device

Haveli needs no account and works fully offline. Your story progress and game settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. The app itself does not back them up or send them anywhere, and deleting the app removes them.

4. Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects privacy, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.
