HAVELI

THE HOUSE REMEMBERS

A first-person horror story inside an abandoned Rajasthani haveli. Nobody has lived here for forty-seven years, but the doors still open on their own. Follow the Rathore family's trail and decide how the story ends.

WHAT IS IN THE GAME

A story in five chapters

Aarav returns to his family's abandoned haveli at night. The Rathores vanished in 1979. The house knows why.

First-person exploration

The story moves through the halls, courtyard and temple, torch in hand. Keys, sealed rooms and a lower door that should stay shut.

The Dai

Something beneath the haveli wears the voices of people you love. Whatever you hear calling your name, do not answer it.

Paintings and journals

Nine family portraits, the caretaker's journal and a mother's diary piece together what happened in 1979.

Three endings

Ring the bell and lock it away, open the door, or run. The script branches to three endings, plus an epilogue after the credits.

Voiced in English and Hindi

Recorded narration carries the story, and the whispers in the dark speak Hindi, with captions on screen.

Before you play

  1. This is a horror game. Expect darkness, whispers, and things that should not be there.
  2. Headphones recommended. In the story, the footsteps stop when you stop.
  3. Read what you find. The journals tell you more than the rooms do.
  4. The aarti is sung at seventeen minutes past three. It is not a prayer. It is a lock.
