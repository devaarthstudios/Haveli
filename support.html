Everything about how Haveli works.

How to play

  1. Start a new game from the menu and watch the introduction.
  2. Use the on-screen controls to move and look around in first person.
  3. Explore the haveli. Find keys, read the journals and follow the story.
  4. At the end you make a choice. There are three endings, plus a short epilogue.

Tips

  • Headphones: the whispers and footsteps carry half the story.
  • Captions: every voice line, including the Hindi whispers, has captions on screen.
  • The paintings: nine family portraits are hung around the house. Each one has something to say.
  • Do not answer: if the house calls your name in a familiar voice, the journals tell you what to do.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need the internet?
No. Haveli works fully offline and has no internet permission.
Is there any advertising or tracking?
The current build contains no advertising or analytics code and has no internet permission.
Is it suitable for children?
Haveli is a horror game with scary scenes and dark themes. Please check the age rating on the Google Play listing.
Where is my progress saved?
In the app's private storage on your phone. The app itself does not back it up anywhere else, so uninstalling the game deletes it.
The game feels slow on my phone.
Close other apps and restart the game. If it stays slow, email us your phone model and Android version.

Still stuck?

Email devyansh.du@gmail.com and include your phone model and Android version.
